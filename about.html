About Let's Count

Let's Count offers free calculators for people who run, walk and cycle. It is an independent website. It is not part of, or endorsed by, any fitness app or company.

What you can do here

Use the Pace Calculator to understand your speed, the Race Time Predictor to set a realistic goal, the Calories Burned Calculator for a rough energy estimate, the Weekly Training Planner to build up gradually, and the Heart Rate Zone Calculator to match effort to your heart rate.

How the calculators are built

Each calculator uses a published or widely used method and shows it on the page, with a worked example and the assumptions behind your result. Calculations run in your browser. The numbers you type are not stored on a server. Where the optional "Explain this result" button is available, pressing it sends only the result figures (never your name or email) to an AI service, which writes a short explanation.

What results mean

Results are estimates for planning. They are not guarantees, and they are not medical, nutrition or coaching advice. If you have a health condition, are injured or are returning to exercise after a long break, speak to a doctor or qualified coach first.

Languages and units

The site is available in English and Hindi. Distances default to kilometres and can be switched to miles on each calculator.
