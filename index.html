Free running, walking & cycling calculators

Answers to the questions athletes ask most: how fast, how far, how many calories and how hard. Each calculator shows its method, a worked example and its limits.

Choose a calculator

Pace CalculatorPace, speed and split times from your distance and time, or a finish time from a pace. Race Time PredictorEstimate your 10K, half marathon or marathon time from a recent run. Calories Burned CalculatorEstimate calories used when you run, walk or cycle. Weekly Training PlannerBuild up your weekly distance gradually, with recovery weeks. Heart Rate Zone CalculatorFind your estimated maximum heart rate and five effort zones from your age.

Who these calculators are for

They are for beginners who want to know whether a goal is realistic, runners and cyclists preparing for an event, casual exercisers who walk or ride for fitness, and coaches who need a quick check for the athletes they guide.

What you need to use them

Only simple numbers: distance, time, your age, your weight and roughly how much you train each week. You do not need a sports watch or heart-rate data. Every calculator works in kilometres by default and can switch to miles.

How to read the results

Results are estimates based on published formulas. Each page lists the formula, the assumptions used and where the estimate can go wrong. Your own results will vary with fitness, weather, terrain and health. These tools do not give medical advice, so speak to a doctor if you have a health condition or feel unwell when exercising.
